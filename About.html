About OSMR Project

What is OSMR?

OSMR is a website concept designed for student music producers, sound designers, and audio engineers. It acts as a collaborative feedback hub where creators can upload their unfinished DAW projects, track stems, or mixdowns to receive critical advice from peer producers with specialized skills.

My Mission

To bridge the gap between student artists and professional industry-standard mixes through peer-to-peer collaboration and community knowledge-sharing.
