Music Production Tips

Quick guidelines to improve your tracks before you upload them for feedback.


1. Before You Upload

  • Check Headroom: Ensure your master bus isn't clipping. Leave around -3dB to -6dB of headroom if you want feedback on the mix.
  • Name Your Tracks: If you are uploading stems or a DAW project file, label your tracks clearly (e.g., "Kick", "Lead Synth", "Vocal Main").
  • Specify Your DAW: Mention whether you use Ableton, FL Studio, Logic Pro, or Pro Tools so others with the same DAW can help you better.

2. Mixing & Arrangement

  • High-Pass Filtering: Clean up the low-end mud by cutting unnecessary sub-frequencies on non-bass instruments (like vocals or guitars).
  • Reference Tracks: Always compare your mix to a professional commercial track in the same genre to balance your levels.
  • Focal Point: Make sure it's clear what the listener should focus on at any given moment (the vocal, a lead line, or a specific beat).

3. Giving & Getting Feedback

  • Be Specific: When uploading, ask for help on specific areas (e.g., "How does the low-end sound?" or "Is the vocal sitting right in the mix?").
  • Constructive Criticism: When helping peers, mention what you like first, then politely suggest areas of improvement.
