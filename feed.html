Feedback Rules

  • Be Constructive: Say what you like before pointing out mix flaws.
  • Be Specific: Mention timestamps (e.g., "At 1:24 the vocals clash with the synth").
  • Check Technicals: Comment on low-end mud, clipping, stereo width, or arrangement pacing.
